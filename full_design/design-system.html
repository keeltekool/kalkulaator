Automaksu Kalkulaator — Design System

Tokens, type, and components powering the Estonian Vehicle Tax Calculator. All values live in styles.css as CSS custom properties, with a [data-theme="dark"] override for night mode.

Colors — Surface

A warm-neutral white/black ramp keeps the calculator feeling clinical and trustworthy. Saturations stay below 0.012 to avoid colour cast.

Colors — Accent & Semantics

A single green accent does most of the work. Blue marks the registration fee (a different financial concept), amber gates informational warnings, red is reserved for inline validation errors.

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans for UI — modern, geometric, friendlier than corporate grotesks. JetBrains Mono for all currency / unit values, locking columns onto a tabular grid.
